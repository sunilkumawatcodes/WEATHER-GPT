# Risk & Travel Decision Specification

Risk bands: 0–24 Low; 25–49 Moderate; 50–74 High; 75–100 Critical.

The dashboard shows the score, band, meter, contributing signals and plain-language action. Production emergency alerts must use authoritative warning feeds and must not depend only on an LLM.

Travel Planner: destination lookup, current weather, rain chance, wind, visibility, apparent temperature, risk band, best nearby forecast window and safety guidance. The result is decision support, not an emergency clearance.
